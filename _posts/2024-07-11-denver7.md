Unfortunately he got some of the details wrong. [Constructive Alternatives](https://constructivealt.com/) does build houses, but for this project they're acting solely as a subcontractor for the exterior block walls of the house. [HomeWrights](https://homewrights.com/) is advising us on how to build the house, but *we* are building it ourselves as the General Contractor.
